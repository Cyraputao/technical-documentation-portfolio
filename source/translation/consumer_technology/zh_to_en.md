## 1. 项目概述（Project Overview）

本项目以 DREO Tower Fan 554S 美国官网公开产品资料为参考，开展消费科技产品技术资料的中译英实践。

中文翻译底稿根据官方英文产品介绍、技术规格、用户手册及 FAQ 内容整理和编写，并非企业官方中文版。

翻译内容涵盖产品介绍、核心功能、技术参数、操作说明以及常见问题，重点练习消费科技产品领域的中英文转换、专业术语运用、技术信息表达和英文技术文档写作。

**一、使用前的准备**

首次使用前，请先检查产品及其配件是否完好，并按照说明书中的组装步骤安装底座。确认底座已正确固定后，方可将风扇直立放置并接通电源。安装过程中，应将电源线穿过底座及机身后部的所有线缆固定卡扣，避免电源线未正确布置而影响设备稳定性。

### 3.3 英文译文（English Translation）

**1. Preparation Before Use**

Before using the fan for the first time, check that the appliance and all accessories are in good condition. Assemble the base by following the instructions in the user manual. Make sure the base is securely installed before placing the fan upright and connecting it to a power outlet. During assembly, route the power cord through the center opening in the base and all rear cord keepers to ensure that the cord is properly positioned and does not affect the stability of the appliance.

**2. Operating Environment and Safety Requirements**

Place the fan on a flat, dry surface and leave sufficient clearance around the appliance to allow proper airflow. Do not cover the air inlet or outlet while the fan is operating. Keep your fingers and other objects away from the fan grilles.

**3. Cleaning and Maintenance Precautions**

Before cleaning the appliance, turn it off and unplug it from the power outlet. Do not immerse the appliance in water or any other liquid. Do not attempt to disassemble or modify its electrical or mechanical components. If the appliance requires servicing, contact qualified service personnel.

### 4.2 中文底稿（Chinese Source Text）

**中文底稿（改编练习文本）：**

**一、风速与运行模式设置**

设备启动后，用户可以通过控制面板上的增加和减少按钮调整风速。按下模式按钮，可以依次切换普通模式、自然风模式、睡眠模式和自动模式。长按模式按钮约 3 秒，可以开启或关闭按键提示音。

**二、定时功能设置**

按下定时按钮，可以设置设备的定时运行时间。重复按下该按钮，可以在 1 至 12 小时之间选择所需时长。设置为 0 小时即可取消定时功能。定时设置完成后，显示屏将在短暂显示定时时长后恢复显示当前风速。

**三、摇头与设备配网**

按下摇头按钮，可以调整设备的送风方向。如需将设备连接至 DREO App，请在手机上开启蓝牙，并按照应用程序中的提示添加设备。随后，长按摇头按钮约 5 秒，启动设备的网络连接设置。待显示屏上的 Wi-Fi 图标闪烁后，按照 App 中的后续指引完成配网。

### 4.3 英文译文（English Translation）

**1. Adjusting Fan Speed and Operating Modes**

After turning on the fan, use the Increase and Decrease buttons on the control panel to adjust the fan speed. Press the Mode button to cycle through Normal, Natural, Sleep, and Auto modes. Press and hold the Mode button for approximately three seconds to turn the button sounds on or off.

**2. Setting the Timer**

Press the Timer button to set the operating time. Press the button repeatedly to select a duration from 1 to 12 hours. To cancel the timer, set the duration to 0 hours. After the timer is set, the display will briefly show the selected duration before returning to the current fan speed.

**3. Adjusting Oscillation and Connecting the Device**

Press the Oscillation button to adjust the airflow direction. To connect the fan to the DREO App, enable Bluetooth on your mobile device and follow the instructions in the app to add the appliance. Then press and hold the Oscillation button for approximately five seconds to activate the network connection settings. When the Wi-Fi icon starts flashing on the display, follow the remaining instructions in the app to complete the pairing process.

## 5. 常见问题与故障排查（FAQ & Troubleshooting）

### 5.2 中文底稿（Chinese Source Text）

**中文底稿（改编练习文本）：**

**问题一：更换手机后，如何重新连接风扇？**

如果需要使用另一部手机控制风扇，请先在新手机上安装 DREO App 并登录账户。确保手机已开启蓝牙，然后按照应用程序中的设备添加流程重新连接 Tower Fan 554S。如果设备无法进入配网状态，可以长按摇头按钮约 5 秒，重新启动 Wi-Fi 连接设置。

**问题二：为什么风扇的显示屏会自动熄灭？**

这是设备的正常显示设置。风扇运行一段时间后，显示屏会自动关闭，以减少夜间使用时的光线干扰。在睡眠模式下，显示屏也会在短暂显示后关闭。如需查看当前运行状态，可以按下控制面板上的任意按钮唤醒显示屏。

**问题三：为什么重新开启风扇后，之前的设置没有完全保留？**

设备具有记忆功能，可以在重新启动后恢复上一次使用时的部分设置。不过，定时设置不会被保存。重新开启风扇后，如果需要继续使用定时功能，请重新设置所需时间。

**问题四：固件更新期间，显示屏出现“UP”是什么意思？**

当设备正在进行固件更新时，显示屏可能显示“UP”。这表示设备处于更新过程中。更新期间，请保持设备正常通电，并按照 DREO App 中的提示完成相关操作。

### 5.3 英文译文（English Translation）

**Question 1: How do I connect the fan to a new mobile device?**

To control the fan with a different mobile device, install the DREO app on the new device and sign in to your account. Make sure Bluetooth is enabled, then follow the device setup instructions in the app to reconnect the Tower Fan 554S. If the fan does not enter network setup mode, press and hold the Oscillation button for approximately five seconds to restart the Wi-Fi connection setup.

**Question 2: Why does the display turn off automatically?**

This is part of the fan's normal display settings. The display automatically turns off after a period of inactivity to reduce light disturbance during nighttime use. In Sleep Mode, the display also turns off shortly after showing the current settings. To check the fan's operating status, press any button on the control panel to wake the display.

**Question 3: Why are some of my previous settings not restored when I turn the fan back on?**

The fan has a memory function that restores some of the settings from its previous operation when it is turned on again. However, the timer setting is not saved. If you need to use the timer again, set the desired duration after restarting the fan.

**Question 4: What does "UP" on the display mean during a firmware update?**

The "UP" indicator may appear on the display while the fan is undergoing a firmware update. It indicates that the update is in progress. Keep the appliance connected to power during the update and follow any relevant instructions in the DREO app.
