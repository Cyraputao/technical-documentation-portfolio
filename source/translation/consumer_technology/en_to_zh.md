"Height Adjustment" 和 "Oscillation" 均属于产品功能术语，分别译为“高度调节”和“摇头送风”。翻译时结合塔扇产品的实际功能确定中文表达，并与项目术语表保持一致。

#### 3. 句式结构与可读性 (Sentence Structure and Readability) 

原文 "Flexible height adjustment and 90° oscillation help direct the breeze" 将两项产品功能与使用效果结合在同一句中。中文翻译调整了语序，将功能名称与实际作用分开表达，使句意更加清晰，符合中文产品说明文案的表达习惯。

#### 4. 翻译策略 (Translation Strategy) 

本次翻译主要采用意译、术语统一和语序调整等方法，在准确传达产品功能的基础上，兼顾中文表达的自然性和用户可读性。

### 5.2 原文 (Source Text)

**节选 1：风扇启动 (Fan Startup)**

**官网原始问题 (Original FAQ Question) ：**

Why won’t my Tower Fan turn on?

**改编答案 (Adapted Answer) ：**

Check whether the power cord is connected to a powered outlet and whether the mains voltage matches the voltage specified on the appliance.

**节选 2：风扇稳定性 (Fan Stability)**

**官网原始问题 (Original FAQ Question) ：**

Why is my Tower Fan shaky and wobbling?

**改编答案 (Adapted Answer) ：**

Place the fan on a flat, level surface. Check whether the fan has been installed correctly according to the assembly instructions. Make sure the power cord passes through all the rear cord keepers.

**节选 3：振动与噪声 (Vibration and Noise)**

**官网原始问题 (Original FAQ Question) ：**

Why is my Tower Fan making vibration and noise?

**改编答案 (Adapted Answer) ：**

Place the appliance on a flat, level surface. Do not insert any objects into the air inlet or air outlet.
