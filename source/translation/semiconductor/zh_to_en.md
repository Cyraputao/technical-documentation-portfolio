## 2. Source Text

在集成电路制造过程中，通过WAT测试(晶圆允收测试)提取工艺和器件性能的信息用于指导芯片产品的过程研发和生产控制。随着制造工艺越来越复杂，WAT测试也变得越来越重要。

广立微WAT测试机是一款功能强大的快速电学参数测试机，为客户提供准确且高度自动化的测试解决方案用于快速和更好地监控工艺。测试机让工程师在半导体晶圆制造工艺的测量中显著地节省时间，特别在与广立微的可寻址测试芯片解决方案结合使用后，其测试效率更高。广立微WAT测试机已经应用于多家领先的晶圆厂的量产产线和研发实验室。

## 3. English Translation

During integrated circuit manufacturing, the Wafer Acceptance Test (WAT) provides information on process and device performance to guide process development and production control in chip manufacturing. As manufacturing processes become increasingly complex, WAT testing is becoming increasingly important.

Semitronix's WAT tester is a powerful, high-speed electrical parameter tester that provides customers with accurate and highly automated testing solutions for faster and more effective process monitoring. The tester significantly reduces the time engineers spend taking measurements during semiconductor wafer manufacturing. Its testing efficiency is further enhanced when used in conjunction with Semitronix's addressable test chip solution. Semitronix's WAT tester has been deployed on mass-production lines and in R&D laboratories at several leading wafer fabs.

## 4. Translation Notes

### 4.1 Terminology Consistency

- **Wafer Acceptance Test (WAT):** The full term is provided at its first occurrence, followed by the abbreviation WAT. The abbreviation is used consistently throughout the remaining text.
- **Process Development:** Translated as “工艺开发” in the semiconductor manufacturing context.
- **Production Control:** Used for “生产控制” as an established technical expression.
- **Process Monitoring:** Used for “工艺监控” to maintain terminology consistency.
- **Electrical Parameter Tester:** Used for “电学参数测试机” to describe the WAT testing equipment.
- **Mass-production Lines:** Used for “量产产线” to distinguish it from “product lines,” which means “产品线.”
- **R&D Laboratories:** Used for “研发实验室.”
- **Addressable Test Chip Solution:** Used for “可寻址测试芯片解决方案.” This is a provisional translation pending verification against official English-language product documentation.

### 4.2 Translation Strategies

- **Restructuring for Clarity:** Sentence structures were adjusted where necessary to make the English translation clearer and more natural while preserving the original meaning.
- **Technical Expression:** Expressions such as “high-speed electrical parameter tester” and “highly automated testing solutions” were used to convey the technical features of the WAT tester accurately.
- **Logical Relationships:** “When used in conjunction with” was adopted to express the relationship between the WAT tester and the addressable test chip solution, avoiding an inaccurate sequential interpretation.
- **Concise Technical Writing:** Redundant wording was reduced to improve readability and maintain a professional technical documentation style.

### 4.3 Translation Considerations

- Technical terms were selected according to the source context and the terminology glossary.
- The translation prioritizes accuracy, consistency, and readability.
- The term “addressable test chip solution” is currently a provisional translation and requires further verification against official English-language product documentation.
